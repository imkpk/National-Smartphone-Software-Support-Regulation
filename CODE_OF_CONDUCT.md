# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in **National-Smartphone-Software-Support-Regulation** a harassment-free, rigorous, and good-faith experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We also pledge to uphold **intellectual honesty**: no fabricated sources, no bad-faith citation warfare, and no weaponisation of this project to harass individuals or companies with baseless legal claims.

## Our Standards

**Positive behaviour includes:**

- Using welcoming and inclusive language  
- Accepting constructive criticism  
- Focusing on what is best for verified public knowledge  
- Showing empathy toward other contributors  
- Correcting errors promptly when citations fail verification  
- Distinguishing personal opinion from stated law  

**Unacceptable behaviour includes:**

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks  
- Publishing others’ private information without permission  
- Fabricating legal authorities or evidence  
- Deliberately misrepresenting court holdings  
- Spam, phishing, or malware  
- Encouraging illegal activity  
- Sexualised language or imagery in project spaces  

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and taking appropriate corrective action. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code applies within project spaces (GitHub issues, PRs, discussions, wikis) and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the maintainers via GitHub security advisories or a private maintainer contact listed in repository settings (when published).

All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy of the reporter where possible.

## Enforcement Guidelines

| Level | Example | Consequence |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| 1 Correction | Inappropriate tone | Private warning |
| 2 Warning | Repeated violations | Public warning; temporary interaction limits |
| 3 Temporary ban | Harassment or fabricated citations at scale | Temporary ban from interaction |
| 4 Permanent ban | Patterns of abuse, doxxing, or intentional research fraud | Permanent ban |

## Legal & Ethical Research Note

This is a legal research project. Debates about doctrine are expected and welcome. **Personal attacks against judges, counsel, or company employees are not.** Critique policies and public documents; do not target private individuals.
