## 1. Strategic objective

**NORM / ANALYSIS:** Build an evidence-backed public-interest case that the Union should **examine, formulate and notify** an appropriate framework for minimum smartphone software support (OS upgrades and security updates) and standardised disclosure—without asking any court to engineer technology or fix a specific number of years as constitutional fiat.

## 2. Theory of the case (working)

| Pillar | Content | Nature |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Digital dependence | Smartphones are primary end-points for UPI, banking, Aadhaar ecosystem apps, DigiLocker, education, health, employment and government services | `FACT` (policy context; prove with official Digital India / NPCI / UIDAI materials as annexed) |
| Industry practice | Support periods vary widely by brand/tier; volume segments often shorter than hardware life | `FACT` (OEM captures required) |
| Regulatory gap | No identified mandatory all-India minimum OS/security support years for smartphones | `OPEN` → treat as `FACT` only after negative search documented |
| Harm pathways | Unpatched devices → cyber risk; early replacement → consumer cost + e-waste | `ANALYSIS` grounded in technical/official sources |
| Comparative | EU Ecodesign Reg. (EU) 2023/1670 regulates OS update availability for smartphones | `FACT` (EUR-Lex / Commission) — **not binding in India** |
| Relief | Process mandamus / directions to executive to consult and frame policy/standards | `NORM` for pleadings design |

## 3. Forum selection (summary) — **not pre-judged**

**Status:** `OPEN` until objective memo completed under `research/forum/` using `templates/forum_analysis_memo.md`.

Both pathways remain under active consideration:

| Forum | Issues to research (non-exhaustive) |
|-------|-------------------------------------|
| **Article 32 — Supreme Court** | Direct fundamental-rights enforcement; national party array; maintainability/exceptionality; risk of relegation to High Court; suitability for policy-process relief |
| **Article 226 — High Court** | Territorial nexus (residence, sale, use, grievances); “for any other purpose” width; mandamus to Union; appellate/SC pathway later if needed |

**Rule (`NORM` for process only):** Do **not** assume the Supreme Court is the correct first forum. Do **not** assume a particular High Court until nexus facts and forum memo are complete. Final forum recommendation requires counsel review.

## 6. Litigation posture rules

1. No prayer that invents an existing Indian “5-year Android statute.”  
2. Prefer **time-bound consideration + consultation + reasoned notification**.  
3. Use comparative law as **persuasive**, not binding.  
4. Keep economic models labelled estimates.  
5. Human advocate certification before any court filing.
