# Manufacturer Comparison Matrix — Phase 5 WS1

## Repository Analytical Artefact

This document is an analytical representation created for this repository.  
**It is not an official manufacturer or Government publication.**

| Brand | Documentation style | Clarity of multi-year OS | Clarity of multi-year security |
|-------|---------------------|-------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Google Pixel | Single support answer | High (stated years) | High (same windows) |
| Samsung | Newsroom + security site + product support | High for series policies | High for series policies |
| Apple | Security releases + vintage list | Low (no fixed N-year table) | Medium–High (release lists) |
| Xiaomi | Product Security portal | Medium (product-level) | High (baseline + EOL list) |
| Motorola | Per-product support portal | Medium | High (per-product stop dates) |
| Other listed OEMs | Support/product site orientation | Low–Medium (residual capture) | Low–Medium (residual capture) |

**Descriptive only — not a ranking or recommendation.**
