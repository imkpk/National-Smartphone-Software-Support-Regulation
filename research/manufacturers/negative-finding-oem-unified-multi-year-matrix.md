## 1. Scope of search [FACT]

Official manufacturer support/security/product pages for: Google, Samsung, Apple, Xiaomi, Motorola, Nothing, OnePlus, OPPO, Vivo, Realme, HMD/Nokia, Sony, Honor, ASUS, Lenovo.  
Access window: **2026-07-31**.

## 2. What was found [FACT]

| Brand group | Official multi-year commitment style |
|-------------|--------------------------------------|
| Google Pixel | Clear multi-year OS+security windows (7 years Pixel 8+; 5 years earlier listed) on Google Support |
| Samsung Galaxy | Series-specific multi-year OS/security commitments (official newsroom/support/security pages) |
| Xiaomi family | Official **security** baseline (≥2 years; model EOL lists on trust.mi.com) |
| Motorola | Official **per-product** security cycle tables on support portal |
| Apple | Official security-release and vintage/obsolete documentation; **no** Pixel-style single N-year table identified |
| Others (Nothing, OnePlus, OPPO, Vivo, Realme, HMD, Sony, Honor, ASUS, Lenovo) | Official domains/support exist; **unified multi-year matrix not captured** as a single dedicated policy URL in this pass |

## 3. What was not found [FACT]

1. A single **industry-wide** multi-year OS/security standard published by manufacturers jointly.  
2. For several high-volume India brands, a **single dedicated official multi-year OS/security policy page** comparable to Google Pixel’s support answer — residual OPEN for product-page capture.  
3. Manufacturer commitments as **Indian law** (they are private policies, not statutes).

## 4. Conclusion [FACT]

Manufacturer published policies are **heterogeneous**. Clear multi-year published commitments exist for some brands/series (notably Pixel; Samsung series policies; Xiaomi security baseline). For others, official documentation is product-specific or incompletely captured as a single matrix in this workstream.

## 5. Consistency

Complements Phase 4 finding of **no Indian multi-year OS legal floor** — private OEM policies exist independently of Indian product law.
