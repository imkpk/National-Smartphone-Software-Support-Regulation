## 2. Official document classes examined [FACT]

- India Code statutory anchors (IT Act, CPA 2019, EPA 1986, BIS Act, etc.)  
- Public descriptions of E-Waste Rules 2022 EPR (CPCB/MoEFCC)  
- Public descriptions of CERT-In Directions / advisories  
- Electronics quality / standards pathways (BIS/MeitY)

## 3. Requirements identified [FACT/LAW]

Authorities operate binding frameworks for, among other things:

- Cyber incident response and related directions (CERT-In / IT Act)  
- Consumer rights, unfair trade practices, misleading ads (CCPA / CPA 2019)  
- Indian Standards and quality control / registration schemes (BIS / MeitY)  
- E-waste EPR and environmentally sound management (CPCB / E-Waste Rules)  
- Telecom policy and licensing (DoT)

These are **real legal/regulatory regimes**. They do **not**, on the materials reviewed, equate to a multi-year smartphone OS/security-update floor.

## 4. Requirements **not** identified [FACT]

**No official publication of the authorities listed above was identified in this workstream that establishes a mandatory minimum period of operating-system upgrades and/or security updates that smartphone manufacturers must provide for devices sold in India.**
