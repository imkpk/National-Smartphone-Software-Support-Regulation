# Regulator Note — Department of Telecommunications (DoT)

## 1. Authority overview [FACT]

The **Department of Telecommunications (DoT)** is a department under the Ministry of Communications, Government of India. It is the central administrative authority for telecommunications policy, licensing, spectrum management, and related public-interest telecom functions. Official portal: https://dot.gov.in/ Access date: **2026-07-30**. [T1]

Technical bodies associated with DoT's ecosystem (as publicly described) include entities such as the **Telecommunication Engineering Centre (TEC)** and related equipment/testing frameworks; exact institutional maps should be confirmed on official DoT/TEC pages when citing specific schemes.

## 2. Legal mandate [LAW]

DoT's mandate arises from the Union's telecommunications portfolio, including:

- Administration of telecom policy and licensing under applicable telecom statutes and rules (as in force — cite India Code / Gazette instruments when relying on specific sections).  
- Spectrum and network-security–related policy functions as published.  
- Interface with equipment-related technical standards / testing frameworks under DoT–TEC public schemes (confirm current scheme names and legal status on official sites).

DoT is **not** the primary product eco-design or consumer-commission authority; those interfaces sit with MeitY/BIS (products/standards), CCPA/CPA (consumer market conduct), and CPCB (e-waste EPR).

## 4. Relevant regulations [LAW]

- Telecom licensing conditions and network-security directions (as notified — **confirm instrument text before alleging specific operator or OEM duties**).  
- Equipment-related compliance schemes administered under DoT/TEC frameworks where applicable to handsets as telecom terminals.  
- **No DoT regulation, license condition, or notification identified in this workstream** that establishes a mandatory minimum multi-year period of smartphone operating-system upgrades and/or security updates that manufacturers must provide for devices sold in India.

## 5. Relevant advisories [FACT]

DoT publishes circulars, public notices, and policy materials on telecom services, security, and consumer-facing telecom issues. These:

- Address the telecom network and service layer primarily.  
- Do not, on materials reviewed, fix multi-year retail handset OS/security-support floors for manufacturers.

## 6. Relevant consultation papers [FACT]

National Digital Communications Policy materials and related consultation processes (as historically published) are **policy documents**. Policy vision statements are **not** the same as a binding multi-year OEM software-support rule unless implemented through operative legal instruments. No operative DoT instrument identified here that converts such vision into an OS-year mandate for smartphones.
