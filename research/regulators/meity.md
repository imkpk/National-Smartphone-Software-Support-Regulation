# Regulator Note — Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)

## 1. Authority overview [FACT]

The **Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)** is the Union ministry responsible for electronics, information technology, digital governance programmes, and related policy coordination. Official portal: https://www.meity.gov.in/ Access date: **2026-07-30**. [T1]

MeitY is administratively associated with national cybersecurity institutions (including CERT-In as a MeitY organisation under the IT Act framework) and with electronics manufacturing / quality interfaces that interact with BIS and Quality Control Orders.

## 2. Legal mandate [LAW]

MeitY's role is defined through allocation of business / administrative responsibility for:

- The **Information Technology Act, 2000** and rules/notifications thereunder (as administered by the Central Government / MeitY).  
- Policy and programme administration for electronics and IT (including Digital India–related programme materials as published).  
- Interface with **CERT-In** under the IT Act (commonly s. 70B framework — confirm exact text on India Code).  
- Electronics product quality pathways in coordination with **BIS** and compulsory registration / QCO notifications as notified by Central Government.

Cross-links: `research/statutes/it-act-2000.md`, `research/statutes/cert-in-directions.md`, `research/statutes/bis-act-2016.md`.

## 4. Relevant regulations [LAW]

- **Information Technology Act, 2000** and subordinate rules/notifications administered under MeitY's IT portfolio.  
- Compulsory Registration / Quality Control pathways for notified electronics (confirm current product lists and legal instruments on MeitY/BIS official pages).  
- **No MeitY regulation, rule, or notification identified in this workstream** that establishes a mandatory minimum multi-year period of smartphone operating-system upgrades and/or security updates for manufacturers selling devices in India.

## 5. Relevant advisories [FACT]

MeitY and linked bodies publish advisories, guidelines, and programme materials on cybersecurity awareness, digital services, and electronics policy. These materials:

- May encourage secure practices and digital inclusion.  
- Do **not**, on materials reviewed, create a manufacturer duty to provide N years of OS/security support for consumer handsets unless a specific notification so states (none identified).

## 6. Relevant consultation papers [FACT]

Consultation papers, draft policies, or discussion documents (if any) on cybersecurity strategy, electronics manufacturing, or product standards are **not binding law** until notified as operative rules, orders, or standards with compulsory force. No such consultation was identified in this pass that, once finalised, would itself prove a current multi-year OS-support mandate.

## 8. Relevance to smartphone software support [ANALYSIS]

| Interface | Note |
|-----------|------|
| Digital dependence | Digital India and digital public services increase societal reliance on secure handsets — **policy context**, not a support-year rule |
| IT Act / CERT-In | Cybersecurity incident and directions culture assumes patchability of systems; does not by itself fix OEM multi-year floors for retail phones |
| Electronics manufacturing & quality | Primary institutional home for product-side electronics policy and standards interface with BIS |
| Gap | No identified MeitY instrument imposing multi-year smartphone OS/security-update floors |

## 9. Limitations

- Public website review is not equivalent to a full Gazette-of-India historical search of every MeitY notification.  
- Programme pages and press releases are not statutes.  
- Full CERT-In Directions text remains annexed under the CERT-In note / statute companion for paragraph-level court use.  
- Residual risk of overlooked niche notifications (e.g. TEC/NCCS or CRO list changes) — residual OPEN.
