# Regulator Note — Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB)

## 1. Authority overview [FACT]

The **Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB)** is the statutory central board for pollution control and environmental quality, functioning under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC). Official portal: https://cpcb.nic.in/ Access date: **2026-07-30**. [T1]

## 2. Legal mandate [LAW]

CPCB's functions arise under environmental statutes (Water Act, Air Act, Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 framework). For e-waste, CPCB implements and guides compliance under **E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022** (EPR portals, guidelines, producer obligations as notified). Cross-links: `research/statutes/epa-1986.md`, `ewaste-rules-2022.md`.

## 4. Relevant regulations [LAW]

- E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022 (parent EPA 1986).  
- CPCB guidelines and portal SOPs for EPR registration and targets.  
- **No CPCB instrument identified** mandating multi-year smartphone OS/security updates.

## 5. Relevant advisories [FACT]

Technical guidelines on e-waste handling, recycling, and EPR compliance. Focus is **end-of-life management**, not in-use OS support duration.

## 6. Relevant consultation papers [FACT]

Draft guidelines may be issued for comment. Drafts are not operative law until finalised/notified as applicable.

## 7. Relevant standards activity [FACT/ANALYSIS]

CPCB is not BIS. It may reference technical standards for recycling processes. Product design longevity standards are not CPCB's primary catalogue role.

## 9. Limitations

- EPR portal data and annual statistics require latest CPCB dashboards for quantitative claims.  
- Does not regulate cybersecurity of live devices.
