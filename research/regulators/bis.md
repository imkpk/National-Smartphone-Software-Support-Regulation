# Regulator Note — Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)

## 1. Authority overview [FACT]

The **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)** is India's national standards body under the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016. Official portal: https://www.bis.gov.in/ Access date: **2026-07-30**. [T1]

## 2. Legal mandate [LAW]

BIS establishes, publishes, and reviews **Indian Standards**, operates conformity assessment, and interacts with Central Government **Quality Control Orders** making compliance with specified standards compulsory for goods. Cross-link: `research/statutes/bis-act-2016.md`.

## 4. Relevant regulations [LAW]

- BIS Act 2016 + rules.  
- Quality Control Orders / Compulsory Registration schemes for notified electronics (confirm current notifications on MeitY/BIS).  
- **No Indian Standard or QCO identified in this pass** that mandates multi-year smartphone OS/security update support periods.

## 5. Relevant advisories [FACT]

BIS publishes standards-related communications. Not a substitute for a product longevity mandate unless a standard/QCO so provides.

## 6. Relevant consultation papers [FACT]

Draft standards may be circulated for public comment. Draft IS texts are **not binding** until published/adopted and, if applicable, made compulsory.

## 7. Relevant standards activity [FACT/ANALYSIS]

| Activity | Status in this pass |
|----------|---------------------|

## 8. Relevance to smartphone software support [ANALYSIS]

BIS is the **primary institutional pathway** for technical standards and compulsory quality orders. Critical for any future ex ante product rule. Currently: **pathway without identified software-support-year standard**.
