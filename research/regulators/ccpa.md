# Regulator Note — Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA)

## 1. Authority overview [FACT]

The **Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA)** is the central authority under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, concerned with protecting consumer rights, preventing unfair trade practices, and regulating misleading advertisements as a class. Administrative association: Department of Consumer Affairs. Portal materials: consumeraffairs.gov.in / CCPA pages. Access date: **2026-07-30**. [T1]

## 2. Legal mandate [LAW]

Established under **Chapter III of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019** (ss. 10–27 structure as published on India Code — confirm). Powers include inquiry into violations of consumer rights, unfair trade practices, and misleading advertisements, and issuance of orders/guidelines within the Act.

Cross-link: `research/statutes/cpa-2019.md`.

## 4. Relevant regulations [LAW]

- Parent Act + Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.  
- CCPA guidelines (e.g. prevention of dark patterns — confirm current PDF title/date on official site) are **soft-law / guidelines** unless the Act gives them binding force for specific purposes — **do not treat guidelines as statute**.

## 5. Relevant advisories [FACT]

Press notes and public notices on unfair practices, e-commerce, and advertising. Useful for enforcement posture; not OEM OS-year mandates.

## 6. Relevant consultation papers [FACT]

Draft guidelines may be consulted before finalisation. Drafts are **not current binding requirements**.

## 7. Relevant standards activity [FACT/ANALYSIS]

CCPA is not BIS; it does not issue Indian Standards. It may influence market conduct through guidelines and orders under CPA 2019.
