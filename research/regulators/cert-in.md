# Regulator Note — CERT-In (Indian Computer Emergency Response Team)

## 1. Authority overview [FACT]

**CERT-In** is India's national agency for cyber incident response. Official portal: https://www.cert-in.org.in/ Access date: **2026-07-30**. [T1]

## 2. Legal mandate [LAW]

CERT-In is established under the **Information Technology Act, 2000** framework (commonly identified with **section 70B** after amendments — confirm exact text on India Code). It coordinates cyber emergency response and may issue directions within its statutory/administrative mandate.

Cross-link: `research/statutes/it-act-2000.md`, `research/statutes/cert-in-directions.md`.

## 5. Relevant advisories [FACT]

Periodic advisories on vulnerabilities, malware, and secure practices. These reinforce **patching** as a security control. They do not, by themselves, fix multi-year OEM support periods for consumer Android/iOS devices unless a direction so states (none identified).

## 6. Relevant consultation papers [FACT]

CERT-In materials are primarily directions/advisories rather than multi-stakeholder consultation papers of the legislative kind. Any draft guidance is not binding law until issued as an operative direction/notification.

## 7. Relevant standards activity [FACT/ANALYSIS]

CERT-In interacts with national cyber security standards culture; product longevity standards remain primarily BIS/MeitY product-policy domain.
