# Policy Note — Digital India Programme

## 1. Purpose

Record official Digital India framing as the flagship digital governance / digital economy programme and its relevance to smartphone end-points (dependence context, not OS-year mandate).

## 2. Scope

## 5. Findings [FACT / PROGRAM / ANALYSIS]

### Document identity [FACT]

- **Name:** Digital India  
- **Vision (official):** Transform India into a digitally empowered society and knowledge economy  
- **Nodal association:** MeitY / Digital India programme structure  

### Substance orientation [PROGRAM / FACT]

Official materials describe pillars including broadband/connectivity, digital infrastructure, e-governance, digital literacy, and electronics manufacturing scale-up themes (as presented on official programme pages). Mobile connectivity and public service delivery through digital channels are core programme themes.

### Smartphone multi-year OS/security-support mandate [FACT]

**No Digital India programme document reviewed in this workstream establishes a mandatory multi-year OEM OS/security-update support period for smartphones.**

### Relevance [ANALYSIS]

| Interface | Note |
|-----------|------|
| End-point dependence | Programme success assumes citizens can securely access digital public services — often via smartphones |
| Manufacturing pillar | Complements NPE electronics manufacturing aims |
| Software support years | Programme vision ≠ product longevity mandate |

## 6. Negative findings [FACT]

Digital India official framing: **no multi-year smartphone software-support duty** identified.

## 7. Limitations

- Programme has many sub-schemes; this note is framing-level, not exhaustive scheme inventory.  
- Press releases alone not used as sole source of programme structure.  
- DPDP Act is separate **law** (not this programme note).
