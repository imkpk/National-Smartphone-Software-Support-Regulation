## 5. Findings [FACT / POLICY / ANALYSIS]

### Document identity [FACT]

- **Title:** National Policy on Electronics, 2019 (NPE 2019)  
- **Ministry:** MeitY  
- **Approval:** Union Cabinet (PIB 19 Feb 2019); notification dated **25 February 2019** (official PDF)  

### Substance orientation [POLICY / FACT]

NPE 2019 aims to position India as a global hub for Electronics System Design and Manufacturing (ESDM), promote domestic manufacturing, exports, standards compliance, and related ecosystem measures. Official text contemplates institutional mechanisms within MeitY for **mandating compliance to standards for electronics products** (standards/QCO pathway — implementation via separate legal instruments).

### Smartphone multi-year OS/security-support mandate [FACT]

**No provision was identified in NPE 2019 that itself sets a mandatory multi-year OS upgrade or security-update support period for smartphones sold in India.**

Standards-compliance language is a **policy pathway**, not an identified multi-year software-support floor.

### Relevance [ANALYSIS]

| Interface | Note |
|-----------|------|
| Manufacturing & ESDM | Handsets are core electronics products under industrial policy |
| Standards | Aligns with BIS/MeitY compulsory registration / QCO architecture (Phase 2–3) |
| Longevity / software support | Not identified as an explicit NPE numeric OS-year obligation |

## 6. Negative findings [FACT]

NPE 2019: **no multi-year smartphone software-support duty** identified as binding policy text of that kind.
