## 5. Findings [FACT / POLICY / ANALYSIS]

### Document identity [FACT]

- **Title:** National Digital Communications Policy, 2018  
- **Department:** Department of Telecommunications  
- **Status:** Official national telecom/digital communications policy (2018)  

### Substance orientation [POLICY / FACT]

NDCP 2018 seeks to unlock digital communications networks for India’s digital economy and Digital India goals: connectivity, broadband, investment, secure and resilient digital communications, and related missions (as set out in the policy).

### Smartphone multi-year OS/security-support mandate [FACT]

**No provision was identified in NDCP 2018 establishing a mandatory multi-year OS upgrade or security-update support period for smartphone manufacturers selling devices to consumers in India.**

Network security and secure digital communications objectives do not, on materials reviewed, equate to retail handset multi-year OEM software-support floors.

### Relevance [ANALYSIS]

| Interface | Note |
|-----------|------|
| Device as terminal | Smartphones are primary access devices for digital communications |
| Security resilience | Policy-level security themes for networks/services |
| Product OS years | Not identified as NDCP product rule |

## 6. Negative findings [FACT]

NDCP 2018: **no multi-year smartphone OEM software-support duty** identified.
