## 5. Findings [FACT / POLICY / ANALYSIS]

### Document identity [FACT]

- **Title:** National Cyber Security Policy, 2013  
- **Issuing era:** DeitY / MeitY portfolio (Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology lineage)  
- **Status:** Official policy document published by Government of India / MeitY channels  

### Substance orientation [POLICY / FACT]

Public text of NCSP 2013 articulates national objectives to protect information infrastructure, reduce vulnerabilities, build capacity, and promote secure computing practices. It frames cyber security as a **national strategic priority** involving government, public, and private stakeholders.

### Smartphone multi-year OS/security-support mandate [FACT]

**No provision was identified in this policy document that establishes a mandatory multi-year period of operating-system upgrades or security updates that smartphone manufacturers must provide for consumer devices sold in India.**

### Relevance [ANALYSIS]

| Interface | Note |
|-----------|------|
| Patching culture | Policy assumes systems should be secured and vulnerabilities reduced — institutional premise for update hygiene |
| End-point devices | Smartphones are part of the digital ecosystem but are not given a fixed OEM support-year floor in this policy |
| Later instruments | CERT-In directions and other cyber instruments are separate (Phase 2–3 notes) |

## 6. Negative findings [FACT]

Within NCSP 2013: **no multi-year smartphone OEM software-support duty** identified.
