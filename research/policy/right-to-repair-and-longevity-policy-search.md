### Right to repair / longevity [FACT]

**No dedicated official Government of India national policy document was identified in this workstream** that:

1. Establishes a **right to repair** regime for smartphones with multi-year software-support duties; or  
2. Sets a **mandatory multi-year smartphone OS/security-update support period** as national product policy.

E-waste and circular-economy themes appear in environmental rule frameworks (statutes/regulators) rather than as a dedicated multi-year OS support **policy** in this pass.
