## 3. Requirements identified [FACT / POLICY]

Policies identify strategic goals for:

- Cyber security capacity and secure computing  
- Electronics manufacturing and standards compliance pathways  
- Digital public infrastructure and connectivity  
- Digital communications growth and resilience  

These are **real policy/programme instruments**. They do **not**, on materials reviewed, create multi-year smartphone OEM OS/security-update floors.

## 4. Requirements **not** identified [FACT]

**No official Government of India policy or programme document reviewed in Phase 4 Workstream 1 was identified that establishes a mandatory minimum multi-year period of operating-system upgrades and/or security updates that smartphone manufacturers must provide for devices sold in India.**
