## 2. Article 37 [LAW]

Article 37 provides that Directive Principles are not enforceable by any court but are fundamental in the governance of the country and that it shall be the duty of the State to apply them in making laws. (Confirm full wording in official text.)

## 3. Research implication for software-support study [ANALYSIS]

1. **FR notes (14, 19, 21, 21A, 32)** map constraints and remedies involving the **State**.  
2. **DPSP notes (38, 39, 48A)** map **policy directions** (welfare, environment)—useful for explaining why the State may regulate, not as self-executing product rules.  
3. **Duty 51A(g)** maps citizen environmental responsibility values—not OEM obligations.  
4. Mis-citing a DPSP as if it were a free-standing product mandate is a validation failure.
