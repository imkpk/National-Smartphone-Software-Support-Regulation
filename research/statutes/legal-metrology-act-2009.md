# Statute Note — Legal Metrology Act, 2009

## 1. Overview [FACT]

The **Legal Metrology Act, 2009** regulates trade and commerce in weights, measures, and other goods sold or distributed by weight, measure, or number, and provides for related declarations on pre-packaged commodities (detailed in rules).

**Official source:** India Code — Legal Metrology Act, 2009. Access date: **2026-07-30**. [T0]

## 2. Legislative purpose [LAW/ANALYSIS]

To establish and enforce standards of weights and measures, regulate trade therein, and protect consumers through accurate measurement and packaging declarations.

## 3. Relevant provisions [LAW]

| Theme | Content (confirm on India Code + Rules) | Notes |
|-------|------------------------------------------|--------|
| Definitions | Goods, pre-packaged commodity, etc. | Scope |
| Standard weights/measures | Core metrology scheme | Not software |
| **Pre-packaged commodities** | Act + **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules** | Mandatory declarations (name, quantity, MRP, manufacturer, etc.—confirm current rule list) |
| Offences | Misdeclaration, non-standard packages | Enforcement |

## 4. Regulatory scope [LAW]

- Physical quantities and packaging declarations for goods in trade.  
- Does **not** currently list “years of OS updates” as a standard declaration field in the general packaged-commodities framework (confirm latest Rules).  
- Smartphones sold as pre-packaged goods must comply with applicable packaging declarations.

## 5. Applicability to smartphone software support [ANALYSIS]

| Interface | Note |
|-----------|------|
| **Disclosure pathway** | In principle, software-support duration could be proposed as an **additional mandatory declaration** via rules amendment—**policy option**, not existing mandate. |
| **Current gap** | No identified Legal Metrology rule requiring OS/security support years on packaging or e-commerce labels. |
| **Complement** | Works alongside CPA unfair practices / e-commerce rules for truthful information. |
