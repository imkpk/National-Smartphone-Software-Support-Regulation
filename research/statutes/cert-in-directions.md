# Instrument Note — CERT-In Directions / Public Materials

**Status tag:** `SECONDARY` for direction text until the signed PDF from cert-in.org.in is annexed and line-checked.

## 1. Overview [FACT]

**Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In)** is the national agency for cyber incident response under the IT Act framework (see s. 70B on India Code). CERT-In publishes directions, advisories, and guidelines on its official website.

**Official portal:** https://www.cert-in.org.in/ Access date: **2026-07-30**. [T1]

## 2. Legislative purpose [LAW/ANALYSIS]

To coordinate cyber emergency response, issue directions for information security practices within its mandate, and strengthen the national cyber security posture (as described in statutory and official materials).

## 4. Regulatory scope [LAW]

- Entities covered by the Directions (as defined therein—typically service providers, intermediaries, data centres, body corporates, government organisations—**confirm in official text**).  
- Incident reporting, log retention, and related security practices.  
- **Not** a product eco-design regulation prescribing OEM OS support years for retail smartphones.
