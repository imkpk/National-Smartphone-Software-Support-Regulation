# Rules Note — Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020

## 1. Overview [FACT]

The **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020** are subordinate legislation under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, addressing duties of e-commerce entities and related consumer-protection obligations in online commerce.

**Official source:** Gazette / Department of Consumer Affairs publications of the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 (as amended). Locate current text via India Code / egazette / consumeraffairs.gov.in. Access date: **2026-07-30**. [T0/T1]

## 2. Legislative purpose [LAW/ANALYSIS]

To operationalise consumer protection in e-commerce under the parent Act—transparency, grievance redressal, and unfair trade practice controls in digital marketplaces.

## 3. Relevant provisions [LAW]

Typical themes in the 2020 Rules (confirm exact rule numbers in official Gazette text):

- Duties of e-commerce entities (marketplace / inventory models as defined).  
- Information display, grievance officers, and related compliance.  
- Prohibitions aligned with unfair trade practices / misleading practices under the parent Act.  
- Later amendments (if any) must be checked on official portals before reliance.

## 4. Regulatory scope [LAW]

- Online sale of goods/services through e-commerce entities as defined.  
- Complements CPA 2019; does not replace product-liability chapter.  
- Does not, by known general structure, prescribe OEM OS update years.
