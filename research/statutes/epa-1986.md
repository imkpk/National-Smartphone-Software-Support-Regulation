# Statute Note — Environment (Protection) Act, 1986

## 1. Overview [FACT]

The **Environment (Protection) Act, 1986** (EPA) is the umbrella central environmental statute empowering the Central Government to take measures for protecting and improving environmental quality and preventing environmental pollution.

**Official source:** India Code — Environment (Protection) Act, 1986. Access date: **2026-07-30**. [T0]

## 2. Legislative purpose [LAW/ANALYSIS]

To provide a framework for environmental protection following the Bhopal disaster era reforms, enabling comprehensive Central regulation where needed (see Act long title/preamble on India Code).

## 3. Relevant provisions [LAW]

| Theme | Typical provisions (confirm on India Code) | Notes |
|-------|---------------------------------------------|--------|
| Power to take measures | **Section 3** (commonly cited) | Broad Central Government power to protect/improve environment |
| Rules | **Sections 6 / 25** (rule-making—confirm numbering) | Foundation for subordinate rules including e-waste rules |
| Standards / restrictions | Empowering scheme of the Act | Implemented via notifications and rules |
| Penalties | Offence chapters | Enforcement machinery |

## 4. Regulatory scope [LAW]

- Environment broadly defined; applies to industries, processes, and materials as notified.  
- Parent statute for numerous rules (hazardous waste, e-waste, etc.).  
- Does not itself list “years of Android updates.”
