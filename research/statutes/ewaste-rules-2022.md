# Rules Note — E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022

## 1. Overview [FACT]

The **E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022** are rules under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986, governing environmentally sound management of e-waste, including **Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR)** for producers of electrical and electronic equipment (EEE).

**Official source:** Gazette of India / MoEFCC notification of E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022; CPCB implementation materials. Access date: **2026-07-30**. [T0/T1]  
Search: moef.gov.in; cpcb.nic.in; egazette.nic.in.

## 2. Legislative purpose [LAW/ANALYSIS]

To ensure environmentally sound management of e-waste through EPR, collection, recycling, and related obligations, reducing informal and hazardous handling.

## 4. Regulatory scope [LAW]

- Producers, manufacturers, refurbishers, recyclers, bulk consumers as defined.  
- End-of-life management more than in-use security updates.  
- **No identified clause** fixing “minimum years of OS/security updates” as of this research note—see negative-finding companion.

## 5. Applicability to smartphone software support [ANALYSIS]

| Interface | Note |
|-----------|------|
| **Mobile phones as EEE** | Smartphones fall within e-waste regulatory imagination; producers have EPR duties. |
| **Lifetime ↔ generation rate** | Shorter software support can accelerate replacement, increasing e-waste *flows*—a causal policy link, not automatic rule breach. |
| **Possible future alignment** | EPR eco-modulation or design criteria *could* be designed to reward longer software support—**policy option**, not current text claim. |
| **Gap** | Rules focus on collection/recycling compliance more than OS support longevity. |
