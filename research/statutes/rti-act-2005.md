# Statute Note — Right to Information Act, 2005

## 1. Overview [FACT]

The **Right to Information Act, 2005** provides a statutory right to information held by public authorities, subject to exemptions, to promote transparency and accountability.

**Official source:** India Code — Right to Information Act, 2005. Access date: **2026-07-30**. [T0]

## 2. Legislative purpose [LAW/ANALYSIS]

To empower citizens to secure access to information under public authority control and to promote transparency in government functioning (see Act preamble on India Code).

## 3. Relevant provisions [LAW]

| Theme | Content (confirm on India Code) | Notes |
|-------|----------------------------------|--------|
| Right to information | Core rights provisions | Applies to public authorities |
| Public authority duties | Suo motu disclosure; PIO duties | Evidence-building tool |
| Exemptions | National security, commercial confidence, etc. | May limit some cyber/industry data |
| Appeals | First appellate / CIC-SIC | Enforcement of disclosure |

## 4. Regulatory scope [LAW]

- Information held by **public authorities**, not private OEMs as such.  
- Tool for researchers/petitioners to obtain government studies, file notings, complaint statistics.  
- Not a product-regulation statute.

## 5. Applicability to smartphone software support [ANALYSIS]

| Interface | Note |
|-----------|------|
| **Evidence gathering** | RTI can seek MeitY/BIS/CPCB/DoT records on software support standards, e-waste data, grievance action. |
| **Not a duty on OEMs** | Private manufacturers are not RTI public authorities merely as sellers. |
| **Research plan** | Templates already include RTI application form in `templates/rti_application.md`. |
