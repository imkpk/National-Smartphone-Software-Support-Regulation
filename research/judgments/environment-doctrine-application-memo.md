# Environment Doctrine Application Memo [ANALYSIS]

## Scope

Map SC environmental principles from Workstream 1 to **possible** research framing of software-forced obsolescence and e-waste—without asserting that SC has mandated OS update years.

## Principles [LAW — from briefs]

| Principle | Cases |
|-----------|-------|
| Precautionary principle | *Vellore* |
| Polluter pays | *Vellore*; *ICLEA* |
| Sustainable development | *Vellore*; *N.D. Jayal* |
| Environment as Art. 21 interest | *Subhash Kumar* |
| Absolute liability (hazardous enterprise) | *Oleum Gas* (weak analogy) |

## Application notes [ANALYSIS]

1. **Precaution** may support *ex ante* policy study of unpatched-device and e-waste risks where serious harm pathways are known.  
2. **Polluter pays / cost internalisation** may inform EPR eco-modulation debates already statutory under E-Waste Rules 2022.  
3. **Art. 21 environment** is a constitutional backdrop, not a software standard.  
4. **Oleum absolute liability** should be used sparingly—poor factual fit.

## Limitation

No SC case in this set converts environmental doctrine into an OEM multi-year update duty.
