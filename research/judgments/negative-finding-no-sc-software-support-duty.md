# Negative Finding — Supreme Court Jurisprudence

## Finding [FACT of non-identification within researched set]

**No Supreme Court judgment was identified in this Workstream 1 set that directly establishes a legal duty requiring smartphone manufacturers to provide operating system or security updates for a specified minimum period.**

## What the researched jurisprudence concerns instead [LAW/ANALYSIS]

| Theme | Illustrative cases in this workstream |
|-------|--------------------------------------|
| Consumer protection ethos | *Lucknow Development Authority v. M.K. Gupta* |
| Privacy / digital personhood | *Puttaswamy* (2017); *Puttaswamy* Aadhaar (2019) |
| Environmental obligations / principles | *Vellore*; *ICLEA*; *Subhash Kumar*; *N.D. Jayal* |
| Digital governance / internet | *Anuradha Bhasin*; *Puttaswamy* Aadhaar |
| Constitutional rights method (Arts. 14, 19, 21) | *Royappa*; *Maneka Gandhi*; *Francis Coralie Mullin* |
| Public interest litigation procedure | *S.P. Gupta*; *Chaufal*; *Bandhua Mukti Morcha* |
| Government / administrative duties | *Jagannathan* (mandamus); *Siemens* (reasons); *Modern Dental* (proportionality) |

These authorities may provide **interpretive context** for research on regulation of smartphone software support. They **do not** establish a direct statutory or constitutional software-support mandate against OEMs for fixed update years.
