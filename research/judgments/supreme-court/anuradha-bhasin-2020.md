## 6. Facts (neutral) [FACT]

Challenges to internet restrictions/shutdowns in Jammu & Kashmir and related free speech/trade issues.

## 7. Issues [FACT/LAW]

Constitutionality and reviewability of internet shutdowns; freedom of speech and expression through the internet.

## 8. Holding [LAW]

The Court recognised freedom of speech and expression over the internet as a fundamental right facet and required legality, necessity, and proportionality for restrictions; suspension orders subject to review (details PROVISIONAL).

## 9. Important observations [LAW/ANALYSIS]

Internet as a medium of speech and commerce; transparency and review of restrictive orders.

## 10. Legal principles established [LAW]

- Speech via internet protected under Art. 19(1)(a) framework
- Proportionality / review of shutdowns

## 11. Relevance to this repository [ANALYSIS]

**Strength: Medium (digital rights).** Constitutional importance of digital connectivity. Does **not** regulate handset software-support longevity; relevance to insecure devices is indirect.

**Not asserted:** That this judgment creates a legal duty requiring smartphone manufacturers to provide OS or security updates for any specified minimum period.

## 12. Limitations

About State restrictions on networks, not OEM update policies.
