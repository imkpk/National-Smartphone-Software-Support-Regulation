## 6. Facts (neutral) [FACT]

Petition concerning conditions of detention and the meaning of life under Article 21.

## 7. Issues [FACT/LAW]

Whether Article 21 includes dignity and basic living conditions beyond mere animal existence.

## 8. Holding [LAW]

The Court held that the right to life includes the right to live with human dignity and something more than mere animal existence (classic formulation; pin-cites PROVISIONAL).

## 9. Important observations [LAW/ANALYSIS]

Foundational dignity reading of Article 21 later used across many domains.

## 10. Legal principles established [LAW]

- Life includes dignity
- Article 21 not minimal physical survival alone

## 11. Relevance to this repository [ANALYSIS]

**Strength: Medium (Art. 21 baseline).** Dignity framing can colour digital participation arguments but is high-level. Does not create product-update duties.

**Not asserted:** That this judgment creates a legal duty requiring smartphone manufacturers to provide OS or security updates for any specified minimum period.
