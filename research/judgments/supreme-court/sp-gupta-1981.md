# Judgment Brief — S.P. Gupta v. Union of India

**Litigation advice:** None. Neutral case mapping only.  
**Pin-cite status:** PROVISIONAL — re-verify paragraphs on SCC Online / official reports before court use.

## 1. Case Name

*S.P. Gupta v. Union of India*

## 2. Citation

1981 Supp SCC 87; AIR 1982 SC 149

## 3. Court

Supreme Court of India

## 4. Bench

Larger Bench (Judges' Transfer case; PIL locus exposition)

## 5. Date

Year of decision: **1981/1982** (exact calendar date to be confirmed from official reporter if required for filing)

## 6. Facts (neutral) [FACT]

Challenges relating to judicial appointments and transfers; the Court extensively discussed standing to sue in matters of public interest.

## 7. Issues [FACT/LAW]

Whether traditional locus standi bars public-spirited persons from approaching the Court for public injury; scope of public interest litigation.

## 8. Holding [LAW]

The Court relaxed rigid locus standi where legal wrong is caused to a person or determinate class unable to approach the Court, allowing bona fide public interest petitioners (as commonly understood). Exact ratio and paragraphs remain PROVISIONAL pending full-text pin-cite.

## 9. Important observations [LAW/ANALYSIS]

PIL is a means of access to justice for public injury; courts must still police abuse. Foundational for later PIL guidelines cases.

## 10. Legal principles established [LAW]

- Liberalised standing for bona fide public causes
- Distinction between private injury and public injury
- Caution against busybody petitioners (developed further later)

## 11. Relevance to this repository [ANALYSIS]

**Strength: High (procedure).** Maintainability doctrine for any future public-interest petition on systemic regulatory gaps. Does **not** decide product-regulation merits or OEM update duties.

**Not asserted:** That this judgment creates a legal duty requiring smartphone manufacturers to provide OS or security updates for any specified minimum period.

## 12. Limitations

Does not address consumer electronics, cybersecurity product standards, or e-waste. Standing is necessary but never sufficient for relief.

## 13. Open questions [OPEN]

1. How do later PIL-abuse guidelines (e.g. *Chaufal*) refine *S.P. Gupta*?
2. What factual showings distinguish genuine public injury from private commercial phone disputes?
