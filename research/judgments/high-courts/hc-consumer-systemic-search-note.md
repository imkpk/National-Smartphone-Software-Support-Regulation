# High Court Consumer Systemic Issues — Search Note

**Not a judgment brief.** Documents Task T165 approach and outcome.

## Inclusion bar

Only systemic HC principles that travel beyond one-off refund disputes—e.g. class-wide unfair practices, e-commerce disclosure duties, product-safety public-law overlays.

## Outcome of this pass [FACT/ANALYSIS]

- Primary consumer framework remains **statutory** (CPA 2019) and **SC ethos** (*LDA v. M.K. Gupta* — SC Workstream 1).  
- No High Court judgment was selected in this quality-over-quantity pass that both (a) has a firmly verified official citation in-hand and (b) materialises a **systemic** consumer principle specifically advancing **software-support duration** regulation.  
- Individual HC consumer disputes about defective phones/warranties are plentiful in secondary reporting but often lack clean public citations or remain case-specific—**excluded** under the inclusion rubric to avoid weak authority.
