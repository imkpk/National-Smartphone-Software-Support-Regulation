**Question searched:** Whether official law establishes a **mandatory multi-year minimum smartphone OS upgrade and/or security-update support period** for manufacturers (product-level floor).

Access window: **2026-07-30**.

## 3. Evidence located [FACT / LAW]

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **EU** | Commission Regulation **(EU) 2023/1670** — binding ecodesign rules including multi-year **operating system update availability** rules for smartphones (and related product categories), applicable from **20 June 2025**. See EU note for operative wording (including conditional “if they provide updates” structure). |
| **Australia** | ACL **consumer guarantees** (reasonable quality/durability) — **not** a fixed multi-year OS table. |
| **Other listed jurisdictions** | General consumer, eco-design (non-smartphone OS years), cyber disclosure, or technical standards — **not** multi-year OS floors as identified |

## 4. Evidence **not** located [FACT]

For **United Kingdom, United States (federal), Canada, Japan, and South Korea**, this workstream **did not identify** an official instrument equivalent to a **mandatory multi-year smartphone OS/security-support product mandate** of the EU 2023/1670 type.

This is **absence of identified evidence within search scope**, not a metaphysical claim that no such instrument can exist unpublished.

## 5. Limitations [FACT]

- Non-English Gazette full-text residual OPEN (JP/KR).  
- US **state** right-to-repair laws not exhaustively surveyed.  
- UK post-Brexit SI watch residual OPEN.
