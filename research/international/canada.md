## 2. Status [FACT]

**No federal Canadian statute or regulation was identified in this workstream** establishing a **mandatory multi-year minimum smartphone OS upgrade and/or security-update support period** for manufacturers of consumer handsets.

Consumer warranty and repair issues are addressed through general consumer-protection frameworks (federal/provincial interfaces) and policy discussions on **right to repair** / copyright technical measures — **not** a verified multi-year OS floor.

## 4. Enforcement [FACT]

Provincial consumer-protection statutes and federal competition/consumer agencies operate in their respective spheres; no federal OS-year product table located.

## 5. Relevance to India [ANALYSIS]

- Illustrates another large market where **repair/copyright policy debate** exists without (on materials reviewed) an EU-style multi-year OS eco-design rule.  
- Provincial variation is a research limitation for any future deeper pass.
