# Comparative Note — Australia

> **PERSUASIVE ONLY.** Australian law does **not** bind India.

## 1. Legal instrument [FACT / LAW]

**Australian Consumer Law (ACL)** — Schedule 2 to the *Competition and Consumer Act 2010* (Cth), as administered and explained by the **Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC)**.

Official consumer-facing guidance: https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/consumer-rights-and-guarantees  
Access date: **2026-07-30**. [T1]

## 2. Status [LAW]

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|--------|
| Binding in Australia | Yes (ACL consumer guarantees) |
| Fixed multi-year smartphone OS/security-support floor | **Not identified** as a numeric statutory schedule |
| India | Not applicable |

## 3. Minimum support requirements (if any) [LAW / FACT]

ACCC official guidance (summarised neutrally):

- Goods come with automatic **consumer guarantees** (acceptable quality, fitness for purpose, matching description, etc.).  
- Guarantees last for a **reasonable period** depending on the nature of the goods — **not** a single fixed calendar year for all smartphones.  
- Manufacturer/voluntary warranties **do not replace** consumer guarantees.  
- Remedies: repair, replace, refund depending on major/minor failure.

**Not identified:** an ACL provision stating “manufacturers must provide N years of OS upgrades / security updates” as a product-placement rule equivalent to EU 2023/1670.

## 4. Enforcement [FACT]

ACCC and state/territory fair-trading agencies; private consumer actions; product-specific guidance (including electronics) under consumer guarantees.

## 5. Relevance to India [ANALYSIS]
