# Comparative Note — South Korea

> **PERSUASIVE ONLY.** Korean law does **not** bind India.

## 1. Official materials reviewed [FACT]

| Source | Locator | Access |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Ministry of Science and ICT (MSIT) public English materials | msit.go.kr | 2026-07-30 |
| MSIT product/technical notifications (e.g. charging interface / device technical standards as publicly described) | msit.go.kr / related notices | 2026-07-30 |

## 2. Status [FACT]

**No South Korean statute or MSIT regulation was identified in this workstream** establishing a **mandatory multi-year minimum smartphone OS upgrade and/or security-update support period** for consumer handset manufacturers equivalent to EU Regulation 2023/1670.

Public MSIT activity located in search orientation includes **device technical standards** (e.g. charging/interface policy communications) and telecom/cyber incident oversight — **not** verified multi-year OS-support product floors.

## 3. Minimum support requirements (if any) [FACT]

| Proposition | Finding |
|-------------|---------|
| Multi-year consumer smartphone OS/security-support product mandate | **Not identified** |
| Manufacturer voluntary security-update policies (e.g. OEM pages) | **Not law** — excluded as binding authority |

## 4. Enforcement [FACT]

MSIT and related agencies enforce telecom, privacy, and product technical rules within their mandates; no multi-year OS table located.

## 5. Relevance to India [ANALYSIS]

- Major smartphone manufacturing/export economy without (on materials reviewed) an EU-style multi-year OS eco-design mandate.  
- Reinforces that industrial capacity ≠ automatic multi-year OS product law.  
- OEM voluntary multi-year security support (market practice) must not be mislabelled as Korean statutory duty.

## 6. Limitations

- Korean-language full statute search residual OPEN.  
- News about carrier breaches ≠ product OS-support law.  
- USB-C / charging technical rules are **not** OS-year rules.
