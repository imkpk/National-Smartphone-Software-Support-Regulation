# Comparative Note — European Union

> **PERSUASIVE ONLY.** EU law does **not** bind India unless India enacts equivalent measures. Do not cite as Indian black-letter law.

## 1. Legal instrument [FACT / LAW]

**Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/1670** of 16 June 2023 laying down ecodesign requirements for smartphones, mobile phones other than smartphones, cordless phones and slate tablets pursuant to **Directive 2009/125/EC**, and amending Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/826.

- Official Journal: OJ L 214, 31.8.2023, p. 47  
- CELEX: 32023R1670  
- ELI: http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1670/oj  
- Access date: **2026-07-30**. [T0]

Related energy-labelling measure (separate instrument): Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/1669 (smartphones and slate tablets) — cited in recitals; not a substitute for 2023/1670.

| Attribute | Value |

## 3. Minimum support requirements (if any) [LAW]

### 3.1 Operating system updates (Annex II — smartphones section, operative text)

From official EUR-Lex text (Annex II, “Operating system updates” under smartphone requirements):

- From the **date of end of placement on the market** to **at least 5 years** after that date, manufacturers, importers or authorised representatives shall, **if they provide** security updates, corrective updates or functionality updates to an operating system, **make such updates available at no cost** for all units of a product model with the same operating system.  
- Applies to both voluntary updates and updates provided to comply with Union law.  
- Timing: security/corrective updates available to the user at the latest **4 months** after public release of underlying OS source (or after release by OS provider / same brand if source not public); functionality updates at the latest **6 months**.  
- Functionality updates that negatively impact performance must be remedied (conditions in annex).

**Definitions** (Annex I): “security update”, “corrective update”, “functionality update”, “operating system”, “date of end of placement on the market”.

**Caution [ANALYSIS]:** Operative wording uses a conditional “if they provide … updates”. Recital (7) states the policy aim that users keep receiving updates for a minimum period at no cost. For court-ready use, annex the full Annex II smartphone subsection and avoid paraphrasing beyond the official text.

### 3.2 Repair / spare parts (context, not OS years)

Annex II also requires multi-year **spare-part** availability (commonly **7 years** after end of placement on the market for listed parts) and repair information access — **resource-efficiency** rules distinct from, but complementary to, OS-update rules.

## 4. Enforcement [LAW / FACT]

- Conformity assessment under Ecodesign Directive procedures (Article 4).  
- Market surveillance by Member State authorities; verification procedure in Annex IV.  
- Circumvention prohibited (Article 6).  
- Review clause: Commission review by **20 September 2027**, including appropriateness of **extending periods for availability of OS updates** (Article 8).

## 5. Relevance to India [ANALYSIS]

| Point | Note |
|-------|------|
| Existence proof | Demonstrates a major market can enact product-level multi-year OS-update availability rules via **ecodesign / resource efficiency**, not only consumer-contract law |
| Design pathway | Product regulation (placing on the market) rather than post-sale tort alone |
| Non-transplant | Indian constitutional/statutory context differs; comparative only |
| Gap contrast | India workstreams (statutes/regulators/committees) did not identify an equivalent multi-year OS floor |

## 6. Limitations

- Full Annex II has separate blocks for non-smartphones, smartphones, cordless phones, slate tablets — this note focuses on the **smartphone OS-update** rules.  
- Consolidated version as of 20/06/2025 exists on EUR-Lex; always re-check CELEX for amendments.  
- Does not describe Member State penalty schedules (national law).
