# Comparative Note — United Kingdom

> **PERSUASIVE ONLY.** UK law does **not** bind India.

## 1. Legal instruments reviewed [FACT]

| Instrument | Locator | Access |
|------------|---------|--------|
| Ecodesign for Energy-Related Products and Energy Information Regulations **2021** (SI 2021/745) | legislation.gov.uk | 2026-07-30 |
| UK right-to-repair / spare-parts regime for certain white goods (as implemented via eco-design measures publicised by UK Government) | gov.uk / legislation.gov.uk | 2026-07-30 |
| EU Regulation 2023/1670 (for **contrast only** — see EU note) | EUR-Lex | 2026-07-30 |

## 2. Status [LAW / FACT]

- The UK maintains an **eco-design and energy-labelling framework** post-Brexit (including SI 2021/745 and related measures).  
- Publicly documented UK **right-to-repair / spare-part availability** measures have focused substantially on **certain white goods and TVs** (as described in official UK communications on eco-design).  
- **No UK statute or regulation was identified in this pass** that replicates **Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/1670**’s smartphone multi-year OS-update availability rules for the GB market as a free-standing equivalent.

## 3. Minimum support requirements (if any) [FACT]

| Proposition | Finding |
|-------------|---------|
| Mandatory multi-year smartphone OS upgrade / security-update floor equivalent to EU 2023/1670 | **Not identified** for UK domestic law in this workstream |
| Hardware spare-parts / repairability for listed product groups under eco-design | Exists for **certain** product categories under UK eco-design measures — **not verified here as a smartphone OS-year rule** |

## 4. Enforcement [FACT]

UK market surveillance / OPSS (Office for Product Safety and Standards) and related eco-design enforcement bodies enforce applicable eco-design rules **as in force**. Scope is product-category specific.

## 5. Relevance to India [ANALYSIS]

- UK shows **resource-efficiency / repair** product regulation without (on materials reviewed) a clear public clone of EU smartphone **OS-update years**.  
- Useful caution against assuming “common law jurisdictions = no product longevity rules” or the opposite.  
- Does not create Indian obligations.

## 6. Limitations

- Post-Brexit divergence is fast-moving; residual **OPEN** whether any later UK SI adopts smartphone OS-update floors.  
- Northern Ireland / Windsor Framework product-rule nuances not fully mapped.  
- Secondary news claims of “UK 5-year update laws” rejected unless tied to legislation.gov.uk.

## 7. Open questions [OPEN]

1. Has any UK SI since 2023 expressly applied smartphone OS-update availability periods?  
2. Interaction of UK consumer-rights acts with software support claims (case-by-case; not a fixed year mandate).
