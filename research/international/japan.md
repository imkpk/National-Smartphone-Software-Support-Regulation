# Comparative Note — Japan

> **PERSUASIVE ONLY.** Japanese law does **not** bind India.

## 1. Official materials reviewed [FACT]

| Source | Locator | Access |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) — cybersecurity / IoT product security materials | meti.go.jp | 2026-07-30 |
| METI interpretative guidelines on electronic commerce (consumer contract interface) | meti.go.jp | 2026-07-30 |

## 2. Status [FACT]

**No Japanese statute or METI regulation was identified in this workstream** that establishes a **mandatory multi-year minimum smartphone OS upgrade and/or security-update support period** for consumer handset manufacturers equivalent to EU Regulation 2023/1670.

METI materials on **IoT product security conformity** and industrial cyber guidelines address **device security postures** in industrial/IoT contexts; they are **not** treated here as a consumer smartphone multi-year OS-support product law without primary text so stating.

## 3. Minimum support requirements (if any) [FACT]

| Proposition | Finding |
|-------------|---------|
| Multi-year consumer smartphone OS/security-support product mandate | **Not identified** |
| IoT / industrial security guidance | Exists (different domain) |

## 4. Enforcement [FACT]

Sector regulators enforce applicable product safety, radio, and consumer-contract rules as in force; no multi-year OS table located.

## 5. Relevance to India [ANALYSIS]

- Shows advanced digital economy without (on materials reviewed) an EU-style ecodesign OS-year floor.  
- IoT security certification trends may be **ANALYTICALLY** adjacent but are not Indian law and not a handset OS-year mandate.

## 6. Limitations

- Japanese-language Gazette search not exhaustive; residual OPEN.  
- OEM voluntary support policies ≠ law.  
- Consumer Contract Act general rules not briefed as OS-year mandates.
