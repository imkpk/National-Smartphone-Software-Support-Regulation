# Comparative Note — United States (Federal)

> **PERSUASIVE ONLY.** US law does **not** bind India. State law is out of primary scope except as noted for residual OPEN.

## 1. Legal instruments / authorities reviewed [FACT]

| Source | Role | Access |
|--------|------|--------|
| Federal Trade Commission — Right to Repair / Nixing the Fix materials; FTC policy statements | Consumer protection / competition policy | 2026-07-30 |
| FTC Act §5 unfair/deceptive practices framework (general) | Conduct standards; not multi-year OS product floor | 2026-07-30 |
| SEC cybersecurity disclosure rules (public companies) | Investor disclosure — **not** consumer handset OS years | 2026-07-30 |
| Congress.gov orientation for federal “right to repair” bills | Legislative proposals — not enacted multi-year OS floors as verified here | 2026-07-30 |

## 2. Status [FACT]

**No federal statute or regulation was identified in this workstream** that establishes a **mandatory multi-year minimum OS upgrade and/or security-update support period** that smartphone manufacturers must provide for consumer devices sold in the United States.

Federal cyber rules located (e.g. SEC incident disclosure; FTC Safeguards Rule for **financial institutions**) regulate **other** domains.

## 3. Minimum support requirements (if any) [FACT]

| Proposition | Finding |
|-------------|---------|
| Federal multi-year smartphone OS/security-support product mandate | **Not identified** |
| Federal right-to-repair as enacted nationwide OS-year rule | **Not identified** |
| Sector cyber rules (finance, public company disclosure) | Exist; **not** handset OS floors |

## 4. Enforcement [FACT / ANALYSIS]

- FTC enforces unfair/deceptive acts (e.g. false claims about support) — **claims/conduct**, not a fixed statutory OS-year table.  
- State “right to repair” laws (e.g. parts, manuals, independent repair) may exist; **not exhaustively surveyed**; none treated here as federal multi-year OS mandate.

## 5. Relevance to India [ANALYSIS]

- Large market without a federal multi-year OS product floor (on materials reviewed) contrasts with EU 2023/1670.  
- Shows alternative policy mix: disclosure, competition, and claim-truthfulness rather than eco-design year floors.  
- Not a model for Indian black-letter law by itself.

## 6. Limitations

- State statutes not fully inventoried.  
- Pending federal bills may change status — residual OPEN.  
- OEM voluntary support policies (Apple/Google/Samsung) are **not** federal law.
