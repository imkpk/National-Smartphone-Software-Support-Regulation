# Programme Note — Digital India

## Repository Relevance

**Why this entity belongs in the repository:**  
Flagship Government of India digital transformation programme; establishes **digital public service delivery and connectivity** context for smartphone end-points. Deepens programme implementation framing beyond WS1 policy note.

**Tags:** Digital Governance · Electronics · Cyber Security · Repository Cross Reference · Consumer Protection

Digital India

## Objectives [PROGRAMME / FACT]

Official vision: transform India into a digitally empowered society and knowledge economy (Digital India portal).

## Programme Scope [FACT]

Connectivity, digital infrastructure, e-governance services, digital literacy, and related pillars as published on official Digital India materials; electronics manufacturing scale-up appears among programme themes in official MeitY/Digital India materials.

## Key Activities [FACT]

Public digital services, broadband/mobile connectivity programmes, e-governance delivery channels (as described on official portals). Sub-schemes are numerous — this note is programme-level framing, not exhaustive sub-scheme catalogue.
