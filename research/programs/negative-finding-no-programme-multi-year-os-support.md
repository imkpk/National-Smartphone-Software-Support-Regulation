## 3. Evidence reviewed [FACT]

## 4. Conclusion [FACT]

**No official Government of India programme reviewed in this workstream was identified that establishes or implements a mandatory multi-year minimum period of operating-system upgrades and/or security updates for smartphone manufacturers selling devices in India.**

Related programmes (Digital India, PLI manufacturing, Cyber Surakshit Bharat capacity-building, e-waste EPR) address digital access, manufacturing, cyber awareness, and end-of-life waste — **not** multi-year retail OS product floors on materials reviewed.
