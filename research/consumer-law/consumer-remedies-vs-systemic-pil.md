# Consumer Law Note — Remedies vs Systemic Tools

## Scope

Map how CPA 2019-style individual/class consumer remedies differ from systemic public-law approaches to a possible **software-support standards gap**. Neutral; no litigation recommendation.

## Verified statutory anchors [LAW]

- Consumer Protection Act, 2019 — product liability (ss. 82–87), unfair trade practices (s. 2(47)), CCPA (Chapter III), commissions. Source: India Code CPA 2019 PDF. Access: 2026-07-30.  
- Companion: `research/statutes/cpa-2019.md`.

## Analysis [ANALYSIS]

| Feature | Consumer commissions / CCPA path | Systemic standards / public-law path |
|---------|----------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Typical trigger | Discrete consumer harm or unfair practice | Regulatory vacuum affecting class of persons |
| Relief style | Compensation, refund, directions in complaint | Rules, IS/QCO, policy frameworks |
| Ex ante floors | Generally no industry-wide OS-year mandate | Could create ex ante minimums if notified |
| Evidence | Individual transaction facts | Market studies, comparative law, e-waste data |

**Conclusion for research:** CPA tools are **necessary but not coextensive** with a national minimum software-support standard. Both may coexist.

## Open questions [OPEN]

1. Can CCPA guidelines alone create de facto multi-year support norms?  
2. Representative actions vs writ process for standards gaps?
