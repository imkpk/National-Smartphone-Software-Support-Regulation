# Consumer Law Note — Support Opacity & Unfair Practices

## Scope

Careful mapping of CPA 2019 unfair trade practice / misleading advertisement concepts to **non-disclosure or under-disclosure** of smartphone software support duration.

## Law [LAW]

- CPA 2019 s. 2(47) — unfair trade practice (broad inclusive definition).  
- CPA 2019 s. 2(28) — misleading advertisement.  
- Source: India Code CPA 2019. Access: 2026-07-30.

## Analysis [ANALYSIS]

1. If a seller **claims** multi-year support falsely, classic UTP/misleading ad analysis is straightforward.  
2. If a seller is **silent** about short support, the harder question is whether silence is a deceptive practice or failure to disclose material information under the statutory definition—**fact- and text-specific**.  
3. Standardised disclosure (BIS/Legal Metrology/e-commerce rules) would reduce opacity without needing case-by-case UTP litigation alone.

**Not asserted:** That every short-support phone sale is automatically an unfair trade practice.

## Open questions [OPEN]

1. Judicial treatment of material omission of digital product lifespan.  
2. Interaction with e-commerce Rules duties.
