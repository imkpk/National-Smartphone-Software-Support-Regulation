# Consumer Law Note — E-Commerce Disclosure Interface

## Scope

Link Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 duties to online smartphone sales and software-support information.

## Law [LAW]

- Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 under CPA 2019 (Gazette / Consumer Affairs).  
- CPA 2019 s. 94 (measures re unfair practices in e-commerce).  
- Sources: official Rules text + India Code CPA 2019. Access: 2026-07-30.

## Analysis [ANALYSIS]

Online handset listings are a primary channel in India. Duties of e-commerce entities regarding accurate information and grievance redressal can **support transparency**, but the Rules (as generally understood) do not specify a multi-year OS support field. Enhancing disclosure may be achievable via Rules amendment, CCPA guidance, or BIS/Legal Metrology fields—**policy pathways**, not existing mandate claims.

## Open questions [OPEN]

1. Current consolidated e-commerce Rules after amendments.  
2. Platform enforcement against incomplete product specs.
