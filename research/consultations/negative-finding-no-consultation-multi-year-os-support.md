## 4. Conclusion [FACT]

**No official Government of India consultation paper or draft regulation reviewed in this workstream was identified that proposes or seeks comments on a mandatory multi-year minimum period of operating-system upgrades and/or security updates for smartphone manufacturers selling devices in India.**

Related digital consultations (DPDP draft rules; AI governance guidelines; BIS general draft-IS comments) do **not** fill that specific product-standard consultation gap on materials reviewed.
