# Institution Note — RBI / NPCI (Stakeholders)

## 1. Purpose

Record why RBI and NPCI matter as **stakeholders** for smartphone end-point security without treating them as smartphone product regulators for multi-year OS support.

## 2. Scope

Stakeholder orientation only. Not banking regulation corpus. Not NPCI scheme rules deep-dive.

## 5. Findings [FACT / ANALYSIS]

| Topic | Finding |
|-------|---------|
| RBI [FACT] | Central bank; regulates banks/payment systems; issues cyber security frameworks for regulated entities |
| NPCI [FACT] | Operates retail payment systems (e.g. UPI) widely used on smartphones |
| Multi-year handset OS duty [FACT] | **Not identified** — RBI/NPCI do not set multi-year OEM OS product floors for consumer handsets in materials reviewed |
| Stakeholder relevance [ANALYSIS] | **Include as context stakeholders**: digital payments assume relatively secure mobile end-points; institutional patching expectations for **banks** (committee evidence) ≠ OEM multi-year consumer OS floors |

### Classification

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| **Include as stakeholders** | Payment end-point dependence; cyber frameworks for regulated entities |
| **Exclude as primary product OS-year duty-holders** | No identified multi-year smartphone OEM mandate |

## 6. Negative findings [FACT]

No RBI/NPCI multi-year smartphone manufacturer OS-support product mandate identified.

## 7. Limitations

RBI master directions not fully catalogued. NPCI circulars residual OPEN.
