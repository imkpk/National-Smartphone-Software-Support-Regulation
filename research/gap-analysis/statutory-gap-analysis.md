## 3. Findings [FACT / ANALYSIS]

### Coverage [FACT]

Repository holds notes on major central instruments relevant to **consumer**, **cyber institution**, **e-waste**, **standards**, **metrology**, and **RTI** interfaces.

### What the repository establishes [FACT]

From `negative-finding-software-support-mandate.md` (VERIFIED as to non-identification within protocol):

> As of 2026-07-30, this research workstream did not identify a dedicated, binding all-India legal instrument that states smartphones sold in India must receive X years of OS upgrades and/or security updates.

Related instruments address consumer protection, cybersecurity institutions, e-waste EPR, standards infrastructure, and packaging metrology — they **interface** with software-support *policy design* but do not, on materials reviewed, create that express multi-year mandate.

### Interface map [ANALYSIS]

| Instrument cluster | Repository finding |
|--------------------|--------------------|
| CPA 2019 + E-Commerce Rules | Redress, UTP, product liability, e-commerce duties — not multi-year OS floor |
| IT Act / CERT-In | Incident response / directions culture — not consumer handset OS years |
| E-Waste Rules 2022 | EPR / end-of-life — not in-use OS support years |
| BIS Act | Standards/QCO **pathway** — no identified IS/QCO on OS support years |
| Legal Metrology | Declarations scheme — no OS-support year field identified |
