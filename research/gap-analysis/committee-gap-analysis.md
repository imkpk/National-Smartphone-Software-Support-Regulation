## 3. Findings [FACT / ANALYSIS]

### Coverage [FACT]

Repository documents committee/subordinate-legislation materials on:

- consumer protection legislative design;  
- e-commerce rules scrutiny;  
- citizens’ data security & privacy;

### What the repository establishes [FACT]

From `negative-finding-parliament-lawcommission.md`:

> No Parliamentary Standing Committee, Subordinate Legislation Committee, JPC, or Law Commission report reviewed was identified as **recommending** a mandatory multi-year smartphone OS/security-support period.

Relevant recommendations located concern other subjects (UTP definitions, data localisation themes, grievance redressal, cyber-fraud posture, EPR/source reduction in e-waste paper). **Recommendations remain non-binding.**

54th CIT report materials note institutional emphasis on **up-to-date / EoL software** in **financial-institution** cyber contexts — distinct from consumer handset multi-year OEM floors (`sc-cit-digital-payment-online-security-54th-2024.md`).
