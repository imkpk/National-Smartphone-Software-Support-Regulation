## 2. Relevant recommendations identified [FACT]

Reports reviewed address, among other things:

- Consumer protection legislative design (CCPA, product liability, misleading ads)  
- E-commerce platform duties, unfair trade practices, data/consent, grievance redressal  
- Digital payments security and online data protection  
- Citizens’ data security and privacy under IT / data-protection trajectories  
- Cyber security and cyber/white-collar crime responses  
- E-waste problem structure, EPR, and environmentally sound management (LARRDIS 2011)  
- Patching / End-of-Life software concerns in **financial-institution cyber-security** contexts (as discussed in evidence in the 54th CIT report)

These are **real parliamentary materials**. They do **not** equate to a recommendation of a multi-year smartphone OS/security-update floor for OEMs.

## 3. Matters **not** addressed / requirements **not** identified [FACT]

**No Parliamentary Standing Committee report, Subordinate Legislation Committee report, Joint Parliamentary Committee report, or Law Commission of India report reviewed in this workstream was identified as recommending a mandatory minimum period of operating-system upgrades and/or security updates that smartphone manufacturers must provide for devices sold in India.**

Additionally:

- **No JPC report** on this specific product-support mandate was identified in the search orientation.  
- **Right to repair** as a dedicated parliamentary report recommending multi-year mobile OS support was **not identified**.  
- **Product standards** discussion in these materials does not substitute for a BIS/MeitY multi-year OS standard (see regulator workstream).
