# Law Commission of India — Search Inventory Note

## 1. Report title [FACT]

This note is **not** a single Law Commission report digest. It records the **search protocol and results** for Law Commission of India publications relevant to smartphone software support and related themes.

## 2. Committee / Commission [FACT]

**Law Commission of India** — non-statutory body constituted by notification of the Government of India, Ministry of Law & Justice, Department of Legal Affairs. Issues reform recommendations in **numbered Reports** to the Government.

Official portal: https://lawcommissionofindia.nic.in/  
Reports index: https://lawcommissionofindia.nic.in/law-commission-reports/  
Access date: **2026-07-30**. [T1]

As stated on the official site (access date above), the Commission has submitted on the order of **277 reports** (site language: research and recommendations via Reports).

## 3. Publication year [FACT]

N/A (inventory covers historical report series 1955–present as listed on the official site). Search executed **2026-07-30**.

## 5. Key findings [FACT]

1. **No Law Commission of India report was identified** whose title or publicly described subject matter establishes or recommends a **mandatory minimum multi-year smartphone operating-system upgrade and/or security-update support period** for manufacturers selling devices in India.  
2. Law Commission reports historically address diverse law-reform topics (criminal procedure, civil procedure, personal laws, commercial law, etc.). **None identified in this pass** as a dedicated product-longevity / right-to-repair / mobile OS-support reform report.  
3. Consumer-protection **statutory** reform in India proceeded primarily through **parliamentary legislation** (CPA 2019) and **Standing Committee** legislative scrutiny (see parliament notes), not via a dedicated Law Commission multi-year software-support report identified here.

## 6. Relevant recommendations [FACT]

**None identified** on the primary target (mandatory multi-year smartphone OS/security support).

Tangential law-reform reports (if any later discovered on e-commerce or unfair terms) must be opened and briefed individually; they are **not** treated as software-support mandates without primary text.

## 7. Relevance to smartphone software support [ANALYSIS]

| Interface | Note |
|-----------|------|

| Repository use | Absence of a LC report on OS-support years is itself material for gap analysis later |
| Weight | Recommendations ≠ law; even if a future report appears, it would not bind until legislated/notified |

## 8. Limitations

- Not every one of ~277 report PDFs was line-read end-to-end; search is **title/subject orientation + targeted theme search**, not OCR of full corpus.  
- Residual risk: an older commercial-law or sale-of-goods report might contain a peripheral sentence — residual **OPEN**.  
- Report numbering and hosting paths can change; cite live official URLs when annexing.
