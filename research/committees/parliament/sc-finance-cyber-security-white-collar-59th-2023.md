# Parliamentary Report Note — Cyber Security and Rising Incidence of Cyber/White Collar Crimes

## 1. Report title [FACT]

**Fifty-Ninth Report** — Standing Committee on Finance (2022–2023) on **Cyber Security and Rising Incidence of Cyber/White Collar Crimes**.

## 2. Committee / Commission [FACT]

Department-related Parliamentary Standing Committee on **Finance** (Seventeenth Lok Sabha). Ministries examined (as per report title block): **Ministry of Finance (Department of Financial Services)**, **Ministry of Home Affairs**, and **Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology**.

## 3. Publication year [FACT]

**2023** (Lok Sabha Secretariat, New Delhi; **July 2023** / Ashadha 1945 Saka per official title page).

## 4. Scope [FACT]

Subject report on cyber security and rising cyber / white-collar crime incidence, including:

- Threat landscape for financial and related systems  
- Institutional responses (banks, law enforcement, CERT-In inputs as recorded)  
- Regulatory gaps as discussed in evidence  
- Observations and recommendations to concerned ministries  

Official PDF: https://sansad.in/getFile/lsscommittee/Finance/17_Finance_59.pdf?source=loksabhadocs  
Access date: **2026-07-30**. [T1]

## 5. Key findings [FACT]

From official report materials:

- Rising cyber and white-collar crime is a systemic concern for financial integrity and public trust.  
- Banks and regulated financial entities have relatively stronger cyber-security postures than some non-bank digital participants (as discussed in evidence).  
- CERT-In actions for enhancing cyber-security posture and preventing attacks are recorded as institutional responses.  
- Focus remains **financial-sector cyber security, fraud, and inter-ministerial coordination** — not consumer product eco-design of smartphone software support.

## 6. Relevant recommendations [FACT]

Recommendations (Part-II of the official report) address strengthening cyber-security and responses to cyber/white-collar crime across the examined ministries.

**Not identified:** recommendation mandating a **minimum multi-year smartphone OS/security-update support period** for device manufacturers selling to consumers.
