# Parliamentary Report Note — Consumer Protection Bill, 2015

## 1. Report title [FACT]

**Ninth Report** — Standing Committee on Food, Consumer Affairs and Public Distribution (2015–16) on **The Consumer Protection Bill, 2015**, Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution (Department of Consumer Affairs).

## 2. Committee / Commission [FACT]

Department-related Parliamentary Standing Committee on **Food, Consumer Affairs and Public Distribution** (Sixteenth Lok Sabha). Chairperson: Shri J.C. Divakar Reddy (as listed in the official report).

## 3. Publication year [FACT]

**2016** (presented to Lok Sabha / laid in Rajya Sabha on **26 April 2016**).

## 4. Scope [FACT]

Legislative scrutiny of the **Consumer Protection Bill, 2015**, including:

- Background and objectives of replacing/modernising the Consumer Protection Act, 1986 framework  
- Misleading advertisements  
- Definitions (including unfair trade practice / unfair contract themes as examined)  
- Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) structure and powers  
- Consumer disputes redressal agencies  
- Product liability  
- Penalties and rule-making powers  

Access date for official PDF (PRS mirror of Lok Sabha Committee report): **2026-07-30**. [T1/T2]

## 5. Key findings [FACT]

As reflected in the report structure and public summaries of the official report:

- The Bill was examined as a comprehensive reform of consumer redress and market conduct tools.  
- The Committee addressed product liability, CCPA functions, misleading advertisements (including endorser liability themes), and pecuniary jurisdiction of adjudicatory bodies.  
- **Committee findings are recommendations to Parliament / Government — not enacted law by themselves.** The later **Consumer Protection Act, 2019** is the enacted statute (see `research/statutes/cpa-2019.md`).

## 6. Relevant recommendations [FACT]

Recommendations (summarised neutrally from the report’s clause-wise analysis focus):

- Strengthen tools against **misleading advertisements** and related consumer harms.  
- Clarify and operationalise **CCPA** powers and related institutional design.  
- Address **product liability** as a distinct consumer-protection instrument.  
- Adjust redressal forum design (including jurisdiction themes examined by the Committee).  

**Not identified in this report:** any recommendation establishing a **mandatory multi-year smartphone operating-system or security-update support period** for device manufacturers.

## 7. Relevance to smartphone software support [ANALYSIS]

| Interface | Note |
|-----------|------|
| Product liability / defect / deficiency | Conceptual pathway if software support claims or product defects are litigated under CPA — **not** a statutory OS-year floor |
| Misleading ads / UTP | If manufacturers advertise multi-year support falsely, CPA tools may be relevant — **conduct/claims** focus |
| CCPA | Class-level market conduct authority later enacted — see regulator note `research/regulators/ccpa.md` |
| Gap | Report does not recommend N-year OEM software-support mandates |
