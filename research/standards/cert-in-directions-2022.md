# Standards / Guidance Note — CERT-In Directions (2022)

## Repository Relevance

**Why this entity belongs in the repository:**  
CERT-In Directions under the IT Act are the primary official instrument shaping cyber incident reporting and security practice expectations for covered entities; they are the main national **technical/administrative cyber guidance** layer relevant to patching culture (distinct from multi-year consumer OEM OS product floors).

**Tags:** Cyber Security · Regulatory Authority · Digital Governance · Repository Cross Reference

## 1. Purpose

Document the official CERT-In Directions commonly dated **28 April 2022** as technical/administrative directions on information security practices and incident reporting.

## 2. Scope

Directions text as published by CERT-In. Full paragraph pin-cites require annexed PDF (PROVISIONAL for fine pins). Not a rewrite of `research/regulators/cert-in.md` or `research/statutes/cert-in-directions.md` — **deepens guidance dimension**.
