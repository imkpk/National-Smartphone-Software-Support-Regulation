# Standards Orientation Note — MeitY Electronics Standards / CRO

## Repository Relevance

**Why this entity belongs in the repository:**  
MeitY administers electronics quality and **Compulsory Registration** interfaces with BIS standards — the main Union **product standards pathway** for electronics manufacturing compliance, relevant as institutional pathway even where no OS-year standard exists.

**Tags:** Electronics · Digital Governance · Technical Standard · Policy Maker · Repository Cross Reference
