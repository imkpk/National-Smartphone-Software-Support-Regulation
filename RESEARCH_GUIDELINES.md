## 1. Workflow

1. Claim a task in the relevant [`tasks/phase-XX.md`](tasks/README.md).  
2. Scaffold or open a memo under the correct `research/<domain>/` folder (`scripts/new_research_note.py`).  
3. Gather **primary** sources first.  
4. Write with labels required by **VALIDATION.md**.  
5. Cite using fields required by **CITATION_POLICY.md**.  
6. Complete Sources + audit trail.  
7. Self-check with [`validation/research-gate-checklist.md`](validation/research-gate-checklist.md).  
8. Update the phase checkbox; note progress in `logs/` when material.  
9. Before marking a **phase** complete, satisfy [`docs/DEFINITION_OF_DONE.md`](docs/DEFINITION_OF_DONE.md) and the phase file’s **Definition of Done** section—then stop (do not auto-advance).  

## 2. Memo structure

Use the domain template under [`templates/`](templates/). Minimum sections: Scope, Verified facts, Law, Analysis, OPEN, Sources, Audit trail.

## 3. Negative findings

If no instrument is found, record portals searched, date, terms, and state a **negative finding** (not “therefore illegal”).

## 4. Parallel work

Research may proceed in parallel by domain; **Citation Validation** must pass before litigation promotion.

## 5. Client evidence

User-supplied grievance numbers and invoices are FACT only when primary documents are present; otherwise “client-asserted pending annexure.”
