## 3. Negative finding (mandatory)

**No Supreme Court judgment was identified that directly establishes a legal duty requiring smartphone manufacturers to provide operating system or security updates for a specified minimum period.**

Relevant jurisprudence instead concerns consumer protection ethos, privacy, environmental obligations, digital governance, constitutional rights method, PIL procedure, and administrative-law duties. These may provide **interpretive context** but do **not** establish a direct statutory or constitutional software-support mandate against OEMs.

See: `research/judgments/negative-finding-no-sc-software-support-duty.md`
