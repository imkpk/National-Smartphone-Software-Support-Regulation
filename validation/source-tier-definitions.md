# Source Tier Definitions

**Authority:** Implements `VALIDATION.md` §4  
**Phase:** 1  

| Tier | Label | Examples | May support `VERIFIED` FACT/LAW? |
|------|-------|----------|----------------------------------|
| **T0** | Primary law / official instrument | Constitution text; India Code; Gazette; authorised law reports; EUR-Lex; official OEM support pages | Yes |
| **T1** | Official secondary | Ministry portals; CPCB dashboards; parliamentary answers; official press information from gov domains | Yes for FACT with date; soft law ≠ statute |
| **T2** | Reputable secondary | UN/ITU reports; peer-reviewed journals | FACT yes; not sole basis for Indian black-letter LAW |
| **T3** | News / commentary | Tech journalism; blogs; op-eds | Background only |
| **T4** | Weak / anonymous | Forums; unsourced claims | No |

## Usage rule

Every table row or research claim that asserts a FACT should record a tier.  
If only T3/T4 is available, status must be `SECONDARY` or `UNVERIFIED`, not `VERIFIED`.
